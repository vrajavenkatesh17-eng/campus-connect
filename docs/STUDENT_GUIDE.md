# Campus Connect — Student User Guide
### Francis Xavier Engineering College (Autonomous)
**Architected by Krish & Ihsan** • Version 1.0.0

---

## 1. Getting Started & First-Time Onboarding
1. Visit **https://campus-connect-fxec.vercel.app/login** on your laptop or mobile phone.
2. Click **Sign in with Google** using your official college email (`@francisxavier.ac.in`).
3. **Onboarding Screen (First Login Only)**:
   * **Step 1**: Confirm your full display name and college register number.
   * **Step 2**: Select your official Department (e.g. CSE, IT, AI&DS, ECE, MECH) and current Year of Study (1–4).
   * **Step 3**: Click **Complete Profile** to access the Student Portal.

---

## 2. Exploring & Registering for Events
1. Navigate to **Events Explorer** (`/events`).
2. Switch between **Upcoming** and **Past** sessions.
3. Click on any event card to view speaker credentials, venue, and seat availability.
4. Click **Register Now**:
   * If seats are available, your reservation is confirmed instantly and a TearTicket pass is issued.
   * If the event is full, you are placed in an **Automated FIFO Waitlist**. If another student cancels, you are automatically promoted and notified.

---

## 3. Marking Live Attendance (`/student/scan`)
During the guest lecture or workshop:
1. Open **Campus Connect** on your smartphone.
2. Tap the **Scan Attendance** icon or navigate to `/student/scan`.
3. Allow camera permission when prompted.
4. Point your camera at the rotating QR code on the auditorium projector screen.
5. Upon successful scan, you will hear a confirmation chime, feel haptic feedback, and see the **Attendance Marked!** green badge.
6. *Notice*: If your camera cannot scan, inform your event coordinator immediately for manual roster verification.

---

## 4. Certificates & Achievement Passport
1. Once an event is marked completed, visit **My Certificates** (`/student/certificates`).
2. Download your official **A4 Landscape PDF Certificate** featuring:
   * Institution header and FXEC official seal.
   * Your name in Fraunces typography.
   * Event title, date, speaker, and JetBrains Mono credential ID.
   * Verification QR code linking to `/verify/:code`.
3. Track your accumulated skill points, activity badges, and leaderboard standing in **Student Passport** (`/student/passport`).
